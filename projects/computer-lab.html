Managing a Computer Lab

Technical lab management involves people, processes, and devices. I help maintain an eight-PC gaming/computer lab, coordinate peer mentors, and support the hands-on technology used by students.

01 / OVERVIEW

The environment

At St. Philip’s College I work with a computer lab containing eight gaming PCs. Students and student workers use this environment for hands-on technology access and learning.

Who and what I support

  • Eight gaming computers
  • Students using the lab
  • Peer mentor student workers
  • Equipment readiness and inventory

02 / PROCESS

How I support the lab

I help coordinate the people and devices required for reliable operation. The responsibilities extend beyond the computers to macOS and Android devices, Android gaming tables, Meta Quest 3 headsets, robotics, and fabrication equipment.

Repeatable processes

  • Maintain Excel-based inventory
  • Create hardware deployment playbooks
  • Write software imaging and installation guides
  • Train users and develop safety instructions

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Documentation and student access

A major part of the role is making sophisticated equipment easier to use consistently. I create guides and usage policies and help students and peer mentors work with equipment effectively.

Other contributions

  • College-wide committee membership
  • Training on robotics and laser/3D printing equipment
  • Raspberry Pi information displays on network TVs
  • Device deployment and mobile device management
