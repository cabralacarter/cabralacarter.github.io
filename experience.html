01 / OPERATIONS

Technical lab management

As an Academic Lab Technician (Cybersecurity) at St. Philip’s College, I support the equipment, people, and processes behind hands-on technology learning.

Day-to-day responsibilities

  • Manage a lab containing eight gaming computers and assist students with technical equipment.
  • Coordinate students in the lab and peer mentor student workers.
  • Manage equipment inventory with Excel and help keep devices ready for use.
  • Create hardware deployment playbooks, device imaging and software setup guides, and equipment instructions.
  • Support macOS and Android devices, Android gaming tables, Meta Quest 3 headsets, and mobile device management.
  • Deploy Raspberry Pi systems to distribute information across network-connected televisions.

A recurring focus is making equipment usable by the next student or instructor—not just completing the initial installation.

02 / SECURITY & NETWORKS

SOC, data center, and network experience

I gained live SOC exposure at UTSA in May–July 2026 and familiarity with St. Philip’s College’s data center and SOC environments.

Security and networking experience

  • Monitor security alerts and network activity in an operational SOC environment.
  • Use Wireshark for packet analysis and network troubleshooting.
  • Practice network design and configuration in Cisco Packet Tracer.
  • Train on VLAN setup and wireless access point configuration.
  • Build virtual machine environments and explore Docker Desktop, Windows Subsystem for Linux, containers, and Pi-hole.
  • Gain experience with cybersecurity hardware including T-Dongle and Halehound.

Additional tools listed on my résumé include Splunk, Microsoft Defender, Cortex XDR, ExtraHop, Armis, Abnormal AI, ServiceNow, Active Directory, Infoblox, and Duo Admin.
